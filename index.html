Behler Engineering

What I built

A full audio engine in C++ using JUCE
Dual-band signal chain with custom crossover, compression, and distortion
Simple UI with complex parameter mapping handled under the hood
I designed the algorithms from scratch. Math-driven, tuned by ear, refined through real use.

Why I built it

I wanted a specific sound of compression and distortion using very minimal UI elements.
I didn’t like the vague “analog warmth” in other plugins.
Behler Engineering is my label for tools I wish existed. Now I make them.

Design & Brand

I designed the interface from scratch in Figma.
Every visual responds to what the signal is doing in real time.
I built a visual identity that reflects how it sounds: clean, modern, slightly aggressive.
The name and UI language are intentionally different from analog clones.
Behler Engineering is my personal label for future tools I want to use.
